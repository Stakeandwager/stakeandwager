Would you put 20 minutes behind a stranger's idea?

Most people say yes to that in principle and no in practice, and I would rather find out which is true here than keep designing around a guess. So this page is both the thing being tested and the test itself. It asks you for something small, and it tells you exactly what sits on the other side of the table before it does.

The wager

Read this page as though it had arrived from someone you have never heard of, then write me a short answer to three questions.

Would you have said yes. If not, where exactly did you stop reading. What was missing that you would have needed in order to commit.

No format and no length requirement. A blunt paragraph is worth more to me than a considered page.

What it resolves

By 16 September 2026: fifteen people will have received this page, and at least six will have sent a written assessment.

Six or more resolves yes. Five or fewer resolves no, and it gets published as no.

Confirm these numbers and dates before sending. Set them now, not once you can see how it is going.

Terms

This is an advice wager. What you send is given voluntarily and I may use it in the project however I like. No ownership, payment, or further obligation is implied on either side. Your name appears on the published result only if you tell me it can.

What you get back

One email on 16 September. Whether six people came through, what the assessments had in common, and what changed because of yours.
